Arca privacy policy

6 November 2026

Your records

Arca stores your Spaces, collections and records on your device. With Arca Sync, the app encrypts collection and record changes on the device and sends them to Arca’s hosted service. Local-Only Spaces keep record changes on that device. Anyone you give an exported archive to can keep a separate copy.

Your account and devices

To provide sign-in, recovery, storage quotas, security and sync, the service processes your account name and email, password hash, device identifiers and labels, public keys, wrapped recovery material, operation identifiers, timing and size information. Wallet and payment card details are handled by Apple. Device authentication is handled by iOS.

Images and files

You choose the photos or files to import. Image fields store a resized image and remove the source photo’s metadata, including embedded location information. In a synced Space, permitted image content and its metadata are encrypted on your device before upload. The service stores encrypted image blobs and routing and size information needed to deliver them and apply storage limits. Choosing local-only privacy removes affected active cloud copies through the privacy-removal flow; the app reports pending removal when it cannot reach the service. Copies already stored on other devices or in exported archives are separate. General file attachments remain in Local-Only Spaces. Arca does not send images to an external AI service automatically.

Suggested collections and AI assistance

Arca downloads a public catalog of suggested collection designs. If you ask Arca to prepare an AI prompt, it sends the description you enter to the Arca service. Describe the fields you want and leave out personal record values. You choose whether to copy the prompt and paste it into an external assistant. That provider handles what you send under its own privacy policy. Arca validates the reply on your device.

Purchases

When you connect an Arca account, RevenueCat processes an Arca account identifier, purchase and entitlement information and supporting device and diagnostic information to offer, restore and manage paid access. Arca uses purchase status to apply cloud limits. RevenueCat privacy policy.

Deletion and support

You can request deletion from the account controls in Arca or contact hi@kawkbi.com. Account deletion removes the active account and associated cloud data. Copies on devices or in exported files are managed separately. Apple controls subscriptions and purchase records. You can manage renewals through your Apple Account. For questions about provider records, backups or retention, contact support.
