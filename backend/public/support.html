Arca support

For help with your records, account or purchases, email hi@kawkbi.com. Include your device model, iOS version, Arca build and the action you were trying to complete.

Keep your passwords, recovery phrase and record contents private when contacting support.

Images and synchronization

Update every device to build 8 or later before using Image fields in a synced collection. You can add several Image fields; each holds one image. With several fields, choose the thumbnail source in Collection Settings. That display preference is saved separately on each device. General file attachments remain local-only.

Recover your records

Keep your account recovery phrase and encrypted archive password somewhere safe. Restore an exported archive into a new Local-Only Space from Settings. Restoring does not replace your existing Spaces. Support cannot reconstruct lost encryption keys or archive passwords.

Purchases

Open Settings → Arca Plan & Quotas to restore eligible purchases or manage access. Use the same Arca account associated with the purchase. Deleting your Arca account does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage renewals in your Apple Account.
